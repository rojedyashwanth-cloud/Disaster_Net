DISASTER INCIDENT REPORT
========================
Report ID      : RPT-2024-0003
Incident Code  : WF-0300
Hazard Class   : WILDFIRE
Severity       : HIGH
Declared       : 2024-07-13 19:05 IST
District       : D06 Forest Edge
Originator     : Forest Range Officer, Sector 11

NARRATIVE
A wildfire started in the dry plantation belt at 18:47 and is being driven by
a strong westerly wind at 46 km/h. The fire front has already consumed 180
hectares and is advancing at 1.2 km per hour towards the Forest Edge township.
Humidity has dropped to 11 percent. Smoke is reducing visibility to under 200
metres on the ridge road. Two forward firefighting crews are attempting to
hold the eastern flank but a wind shift at 19:40 pushed them back.

DAMAGE ASSESSMENT
- 6,800 people live inside the current danger zone.
- A power outage has cut electricity to the township and the water pump station.
- The highway is closed in both directions due to smoke.
- No casualties reported. Evacuation of the northern hamlets is 60 percent done.

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
1. Complete evacuation of the Forest Edge township before 23:00.
2. Request air support for water bombing on the southern flank.
3. Open two relief camps with 1,200 blanket sets.
4. Warn hospitals about a possible respiratory surge.

STATUS: active fire front. Red warning in force. Evacuation continuing.

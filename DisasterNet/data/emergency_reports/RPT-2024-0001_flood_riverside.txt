DISASTER INCIDENT REPORT
========================
Report ID      : RPT-2024-0001
Incident Code  : FL-0100
Hazard Class   : FLOOD
Severity       : CRITICAL
Declared       : 2024-07-14 03:12 IST
District       : D01 Riverside
Originator     : District Control Room, Riverside

NARRATIVE
Continuous rainfall over the upper catchment has pushed the river gauge at
Riverside Bridge past the critical flood threshold of 9.5 metres. Two levees
downstream of the Old Market breach at 02:40. Water is entering low lying
residential blocks 4, 5 and 6 at roughly 30 cm per hour. A power outage has
disabled the East Pump Station, so flooding is worsening faster than the
forecast assumed.

DAMAGE ASSESSMENT
- 1,450 households affected in blocks 4, 5, 6 and 9.
- Riverside Bridge remains open to emergency vehicles only.
- Water supply to 3,000 residents is contaminated and must not be used.
- No casualties reported so far; two rescue teams are en route.

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
1. Evacuate blocks 4 and 5 to the Riverside School shelter before 05:00.
2. Deploy rescue teams T-01 and T-04 with boats for block 6.
3. Request 900 water purification tablets and 400 medical kits.
4. Warn the hospital of a probable 40 bed surge.

STATUS: escalating. A flood warning remains in force for the whole district.

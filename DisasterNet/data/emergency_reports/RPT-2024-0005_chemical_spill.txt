DISASTER INCIDENT REPORT
========================
Report ID      : RPT-2024-0005
Incident Code  : CS-0500
Hazard Class   : CHEMICAL SPILL
Severity       : MODERATE
Declared       : 2024-07-14 09:26 IST
District       : D03 Industrial Park
Originator     : Plant Safety Officer, Unit 7

NARRATIVE
A tank rupture at Unit 7 has released an estimated 14,000 litres of industrial
solvent into the drainage channel at 09:05. The spill is moving towards the
Mithi river intake and the smell of solvent has been reported in the eastern
quarter. Firefighters have deployed absorbent booms and are pumping the
affected channel. A fire risk exists if the solvent reaches the still water
near the loading bay.

DAMAGE ASSESSMENT
- 1,100 residents in the eastern quarter advised to keep windows shut.
- The municipal water intake is shut down as a precaution.
- No injuries reported. Two workers treated for mild chemical exposure.
- Minor soil contamination around the tank farm.

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
1. Keep the river intake closed until laboratory results are clear.
2. Distribute 800 water packets to the eastern quarter.
3. Deploy rescue teams T-08 for decontamination support.
4. Monitor air quality at three schools.

STATUS: contained. Recovery and monitoring continuing.

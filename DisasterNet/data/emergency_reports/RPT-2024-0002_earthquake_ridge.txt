DISASTER INCIDENT REPORT
========================
Report ID      : RPT-2024-0002
Incident Code  : EQ-0200
Hazard Class   : EARTHQUAKE
Severity       : CRITICAL
Declared       : 2024-07-14 04:48 IST
District       : D04 Hill Ridge
Originator     : Seismic Observatory Network

NARRATIVE
A magnitude 6.1 earthquake with an epicentre 11 km north of Hill Ridge struck at
04:41 IST. Shaking lasted 38 seconds. The intensity at the Ridge road corridor
was estimated at VIII on the Mercalli scale. A landslide has taken out the
Hill Ridge approach road and there are reports of a bridge collapse at the
Chandpur crossing. Cellular service is intermittent across the district.

DAMAGE ASSESSMENT
- The Midtown Hospital sustained structural damage and is operating on backup
  power. Triage is being conducted in the courtyard.
- 22 homes in the Hill Ridge colony are destroyed or unsafe.
- The main water trunk line is ruptured near Chandpur.
- 3,200 people are stranded on the far side of the landslide.

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
1. Deploy rescue teams T-02, T-03 and T-06 for urban search and rescue.
2. Airlift requested for two heavy medical teams.
3. Establish a triage centre at Midtown School.
4. Issue an aftershock warning; expect further collapse for 48 hours.

STATUS: aftershock sequence continuing. Aftershock warning in force.

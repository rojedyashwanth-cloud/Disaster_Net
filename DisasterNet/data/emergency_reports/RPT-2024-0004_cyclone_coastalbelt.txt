DISASTER INCIDENT REPORT
========================
Report ID      : RPT-2024-0004
Incident Code  : CY-0400
Hazard Class   : CYCLONE
Severity       : CRITICAL
Declared       : 2024-07-14 01:30 IST
District       : D08 Coastal Belt
Originator     : Meteorological Centre, Coastal Belt

NARRATIVE
Cyclone Meera has made landfall 30 km east of the Coastal Belt at 01:05 with a
sustained wind speed of 178 km/h and gusts reaching 240 km/h. A storm surge of
3.6 metres is expected along the eastern shoreline between 03:00 and 06:00.
The seawall at Marina Point has been overtopped. A power outage affected the
entire district at 01:12 and the emergency operations centre is now running on
generator power only.

DAMAGE ASSESSMENT
- 4,100 households are without electricity; restoration will take days.
- The Marina Point seawall breach threatens 900 homes.
- Fishing vessels at the harbour have broken moorings.
- 2,700 people required shelter in 11 cyclone shelters.

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
1. Keep all shelters open and staff them with medical volunteers.
2. Deploy rescue teams T-05 and T-07 for the Marina Point breach.
3. Request 1,500 blanket sets, 600 medical kits and 2,000 water packets.
4. Suspend all harbour operations until the storm passes.

STATUS: landfall complete. Severe winds continuing. Storm surge imminent.
